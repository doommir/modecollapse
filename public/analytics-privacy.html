Optional traffic analytics

If you choose Allow analytics, NovaPath uses Google Analytics to count visits. Only reviewed public pages are counted; private app pages are excluded.

Google receives the site hostname and a public or general page label, plus standard browser and session information. We exclude query strings, fragments, page titles, referring pages, page contents, form contents, searches and account identifiers. Advertising features and cross-site identification are disabled.

Analytics stays off until you allow it and stays off when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Cookies apply only to this site and expire after 30 days without extending on each visit. Your consent choice lasts up to 180 days.

Use Privacy choices on a measured page to decline or withdraw consent. This stops future collection and removes this site's analytics cookies; it does not delete previously collected data.
